# Analyse lexical relationships in Tungusic and Mongolic corpora

Work from the requested varieties, source-paired forms and meanings, and the user’s authorized merge scope. Keep decisions, evidence and coverage in one offline register. A family is a hypothesis that distinct lexemes share a lexical root. An inferred root can support membership even when no free base is attested. Establish which variety, form, sense or compound constituent each judgment concerns.

## Search for families and lexical identity together

Use one search for formal variants, inflections and common roots. Declare the routes available in this corpus. These may include written forms, printed pronunciations, paradigms, recurring sound or spelling correspondences, meanings, compounds and explicit references. A complete verified group of nested headwords can be searched early when that route is in scope. Printed arrangement supplies leads. Preserve each source’s form–meaning pairings even where entries have already been merged.

Combine repeated retrieval hits for a form pair and retain the routes that found it. Adjudicate each hypothesis at the relevant sense, component and direction. A rejection of identity can coexist with supported root sharing. For an accepted comparison, choose the warranted outcome:

| Outcome | Evidence and editorial effect |
|---|---|
| Word family membership | Distinct lexemes share a supported root. Retain separate entries and assess each membership. |
| Merge entry formal variant | Alternative forms represent one lexeme. Preserve all source forms, meanings and locators under the surviving identity. |
| Merge entry inflected form | The attested form realizes a grammatical distinction of the same lexeme. Preserve the surface form, its source meaning and the supported grammatical analysis. |

Look for attested paradigms and stem alternations before interpreting an ending. A recurring suffix, a similar gloss or a plausible semantic story alone is weak evidence for a common lexical root. Use compound constituents when supported, even if other parts remain unexplained. Review each affected family before adding or merging members. A compound may belong to a family for each constituent. Shared membership alone gives no grounds to merge those families. Preserve unaffected membership confidence and reassess changed hypotheses.

Set search parameters using the corpus’s size, variation, independent root evidence and observed counterexamples. Derivatives, repeated attestations and matches inferred solely from the pattern do not independently confirm it. Keep linguistic confidence, supporting evidence and editorial review distinct. See [judging comparisons and search coverage](references/analysis-workflow.md).

For cross-variety inherited cognacy, compare the relevant family roots or surviving singletons using regular correspondences and compatible meanings. Weigh borrowing and homophony as alternatives. Keep witness forms and sense restrictions, especially when one entry belongs to several families. A cross-variety comparison leaves each variety’s lexical inventory distinct.

## Finish family work and screen loans when requested

Complete declared routes throughout the corpus, resolve their candidates, then review the remaining singleton lexemes once and resolve any new candidates. When the search reveals productive patterns, offer a further pass over residual items against the full comparison inventory. Run already authorized passes directly. If gains remain substantial, offer successive passes and report the new supported relations and newly matched items by comparison until returns diminish. Family-only work can finish here.

When loans are requested, use the resulting families and singletons. Inspect each family as a unit and every member in its own form and sense scope. Examine the whole lexical shape, recipient morphology, historical and regional synonyms, competing donors and possible intermediary languages. Use donor dictionaries to establish the proposed model’s form and meaning, then assess how well it explains the recipient word. Consult the [Chinese and Russian donor catalogues](references/areal-comparisons.md) as comparative cases.

Assess borrowing likelihood, donor identification, direction and route separately. Record a supported borrowing judgment even when the exact donor spelling or route remains unknown. State exactly which component, senses and member-specific models a proposal covers. When a printed source names a donor and a later analysis favours an intermediary or a narrower component, keep the original attribution and attach the qualification to that claim. Check any suspected transcription problem against the printed witness. Return a supported source correction to the import workflow. If new loan evidence changes a family judgment, reopen the affected grouping and its dependent claims.

## Work efficiently and deliver

For long tasks, ask the user to allow parallel processing with three subagents unless already authorized. Assign distinct scopes and reconcile their judgments centrally. Batch related lookups and reuse verified evidence. Increase batch size when repeated setup or verification takes a large share of the time. Smaller batches suit complex comparisons. Record the coverage actually achieved, the unresolved limits and the exact next action.

Report surviving lexemes, distinct roots or families, memberships, merges, and any group loan proposals and applicable member claims separately. Complete the agreed corpus offline. Before an authorized publication, compare the proposed release with the accepted judgments intended for the destination, including their endpoints, confidence and scope. Reconcile an unexpectedly empty export, preserve intervening edits and source claims, and verify the published result through the destination’s guarded workflow.
