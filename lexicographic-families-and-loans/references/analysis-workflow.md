# Judging lexical comparisons

## Verified source groups

Use the dictionary’s conventions to distinguish an independent subentry, an alternative or inflected form, a fixed expression and a usage example. Read continuations across pages before treating a group as complete. Compare the relevant head, children, siblings, explicit reference targets and supported constituents together. An example can attest a form or grammatical use at a particular span. Preserve the whole example and translation when anchoring that occurrence.

Reuse these local decisions during the corpus-wide search. Missing forward targets can remain pending until the relevant evidence is available. New readings or a change in sense attachment can reopen a scoped judgment. Finishing a group establishes coverage of that group through the source-structure route alone.

## Endpoints, evidence and confidence

Record the exact forms, entry and sense identities, retrieval evidence, judgment and reason. For a proposed merger, identify the retained entry or base and the absorbed entry or inflected form. If the base is unattested, record the supported inflectional analysis without creating a lemma for the merger. An inflectional gloss may explain the surface form without adding a lexical sense. Check whether another same-spelling form, a second sense, or a different constituent calls for a separate comparison. Compare unassigned entries against assigned members as well as against one another.

When a glossless entry has an unambiguous reference such as 同, use the target meaning within the scope specified by the source. Cite the target entry and any printed sense number. Assess a possible merger separately.

Accept supported hypotheses with qualitative confidence. Reject an unsupported hypothesis at the scope actually examined and keep unexamined work open. An earlier rejection can be reconsidered when evidence changes. The following scale is useful when the project uses graded assessments.

| Assessment | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| Certain | The linguistic evidence is convincing. Independent documentation and editorial review are recorded separately. |
| Probable | The analysis is persuasive, with a material uncertainty. |
| Possible | The hypothesis is credible, with substantial uncertainty about the correspondence, meaning or model. |
| Unlikely | The comparison has a specific evidential basis but is weak. Record the reason if retaining it is informative. |
| Unspecified | The source or earlier record supplies no assessment. Preserve that distinction from an analyst’s judgment. |

## Establishing patterns

Test spelling variation against actual pronunciations where available. For a morphological construction, identify its grammatical function, the contexts in which it occurs, and independent lexical roots showing the alternation. In suffix-rich material, a shared ending may retrieve many unrelated stems. Inspect internal roots, possible stem alternations and compound boundaries. Keep homonyms and disjoint senses distinguishable throughout retrieval.

Choose a working replication criterion after inspecting the number of independent roots, the distribution across meanings and sources, and the competing explanations. For sparse data, a tentative pattern may justify searching more widely. For frequent short sequences, inspect counterexamples and require enough independent support to distinguish the pattern from coincidence. Document the basis and revise it if a family merger changes the independent evidence. A broader well-attested pattern lends little support to a narrower correspondence whose examples all come from one root.

## Interpreting coverage

Report the inventory and routes searched, the candidates resolved, and the new findings from each additional pass.

In loan work, distinguish the number of borrowing hypotheses from the number of members carrying the proposed borrowed base. A derivative may inherit the component while developing its own meaning or taking a different full compound model. Check each affected member and retain explicit exceptions. A completed screen without a proposal leaves the ultimate origin open.
